# Visual principles

The current visual direction is dark, panel-based, and intentionally restrained. It uses a small number of strong accents and keeps the house feeling present without turning the page into a design showcase.

## What is already established

- Dark background surfaces
- Panel-based layout blocks
- Lime and pink accent use
- Thin borders and soft hierarchy
- Editorial kicker labels
- Resident and guestbook card patterns
- Visual restraint
- Existing logo and visual identity

## How it shows up now

- The page relies on a near-black background with subtle texture and noise.
- Sections are grouped with thin lines, panels, and card surfaces instead of heavy containers.
- Lime is used as the primary bright accent, with pink used as a secondary signal color.
- Editorial kicker labels mark sections with small uppercase labels and short lines.
- Resident and guestbook cards share the same general treatment: border, soft fill, subtle shadow, and compact spacing.
- The visual hierarchy stays soft. Headlines are strong, but surrounding labels, captions, and data strips stay quiet.

## Preserve these patterns

- Preserve the existing logo and visual identity.
- Keep the dark background language.
- Keep accents limited and purposeful.
- Keep cards, panels, and dividers thin and clean.
- Keep the layout editorial and content-led.

## Avoid

- Do not propose a new visual identity.
- Do not add decorative systems that compete with the content.
- Do not replace the current tone with a polished corporate surface.
